Student Housing

Accommodation

Safe, comfortable, and affordable student housing.

We offer on-campus accommodation for undergraduate and postgraduate students, with options to suit different budgets and preferences. All residences are secure, well-maintained, and within walking distance of academic buildings.

Standard Residence Halls

  • Shared rooms (2–4 students)
  • Shared bathrooms and common rooms
  • 24/7 security and resident advisers
  • Utilities included

Premium Residence Halls

  • Single or twin rooms
  • Ensuite bathrooms
  • Study areas and shared kitchens
  • High-speed internet

Accommodation fees and room availability are confirmed on request. Contact housing@kiu.edu.ng for details.
