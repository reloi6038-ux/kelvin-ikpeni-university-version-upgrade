Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions.

How do I apply for admission?

Visit our Apply Online page, complete the form, and submit. You'll receive a reference number instantly to track your application status.

What are the entry requirements?

Entry requirements vary by level and programme. See our Entry Requirements page for full details.

How much is tuition?

Tuition varies by level and programme. Indicative ranges are on our Tuition & Fees page. Exact fees are confirmed on your admission letter.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. We offer merit scholarships, need-based grants, international scholarships, and research funding. See our Scholarships page.

How do I check my application status?

Use your reference number on our Check Status page.

Can international students apply?

Absolutely. See our International Students page for visa support, scholarships, and application guidance.

Do you offer online programmes?

Yes. See our Online Learning page for flexible study options.

Is accommodation available?

Yes. See our Accommodation page for details.

How can I contact the university?

Visit our Contact page or email info@kiu.edu.ng.
