Entry Requirements

What you need to qualify for study at each level.

Undergraduate

  • Five (5) O'Level credits including English Language and Mathematics
  • Relevant subjects for the chosen programme
  • Acceptable UTME/JAMB score
  • Minimum age of 16 at time of admission
  • Direct entry candidates: A-Level, ND, NCE or equivalent

Postgraduate (Master's)

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
  • Minimum of Second Class Lower (2:2) or equivalent
  • NYSC discharge certificate (for Nigerian applicants)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Relevant professional experience (for MBA and similar)

Doctoral (Ph.D.)

  • Master's degree in a relevant field
  • Minimum CGPA of 3.5 on a 5-point scale (or equivalent)
  • Research proposal in your area of interest
  • Academic transcripts and publications
  • Two academic referees

International Applicants

  • Qualifications equivalent to Nigerian requirements
  • English proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, or equivalent)
  • Valid passport
  • Student visa (support provided after admission)
  • Certified translations for non-English documents
