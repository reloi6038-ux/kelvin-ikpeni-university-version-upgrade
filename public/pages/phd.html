Doctoral Study at KIU

Conduct Research With Real Impact

Our doctoral programmes are built around supervised, independent research. You'll work with distinguished faculty, access world-class facilities, and join a community committed to addressing the most pressing challenges of our time.

  • Ph.D. and M.Phil. research degrees
  • Expert supervision across all faculties
  • Research centres and laboratory facilities
  • Conference funding and publication support
  • Competitive doctoral scholarships available
