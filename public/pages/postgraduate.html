Postgraduate at KIU

Sharpen Your Expertise. Expand Your Impact.

Our postgraduate programmes are designed for professionals and scholars ready to deepen their knowledge, conduct meaningful research, and lead in their fields. Whether you're advancing your career or preparing for doctoral study, you'll find a rigorous, supportive environment.

  • Taught master's and research master's pathways
  • Full-time, part-time, and online options
  • Supervision by distinguished scholars
  • Research centres and industry partnerships
  • Scholarships and research grants available
