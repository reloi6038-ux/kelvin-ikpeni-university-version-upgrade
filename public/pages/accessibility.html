Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to inclusive digital experiences.

Kelvin Ikpeni University is committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidelines.

What We Do

  • Semantic HTML and proper heading structure
  • Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
  • Visible focus indicators on links, buttons, and form fields
  • Sufficient colour contrast throughout
  • Alternative text on meaningful images
  • Responsive layouts that work at all zoom levels
  • Respect for "prefers-reduced-motion" settings

Known Limitations

Some third-party content (embedded media, external documents) may not fully meet our accessibility standards. We are working to address these gaps.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact accessibility@kiu.edu.ng. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
