A Home Away From Home

Kelvin Ikpeni University welcomes students from across Africa and the world. Our International Office supports you through every stage — from application and visa processing to arrival, orientation, and academic success.

  • Dedicated international admissions support
  • Student visa guidance and documentation
  • Airport pickup and orientation
  • On-campus accommodation guaranteed
  • Scholarships and financial aid for international students

How to Apply

International Application Steps

  1. 01

    Check Eligibility

    Ensure your qualifications match KIU's entry requirements for your chosen level.

  2. 02

    Submit Application

    Apply online and upload certified translations of documents if not in English.

  3. 03

    Receive Admission Offer

    Successful applicants receive an official offer letter from KIU.

  4. 04

    Apply for Visa

    Use your offer letter and our guidance to apply for a Nigerian student visa.

  5. 05

    Travel & Arrival

    Let us know your arrival details — we provide airport pickup and orientation.
