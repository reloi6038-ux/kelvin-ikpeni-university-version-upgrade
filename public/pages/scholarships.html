Scholarships & Financial Aid

We believe talent should never be limited by circumstance. Over ₦500 million is awarded annually.

Our Commitment

Supporting Outstanding Students

KIU offers a range of scholarships and grants to help students from all backgrounds access world-class education.

Merit Scholarships

Awarded to applicants with outstanding academic records. Up to 100% tuition waiver for top-performing candidates.

  • Based on O'Level and JAMB results
  • Renewable annually with good standing

Need-Based Grants

For students from low-income households. Verified through documentation and interview.

  • Up to 60% tuition support
  • Confidential application process

International Scholarships

For exceptional applicants from across Africa and beyond. Supports tuition and living costs.

  • Partial to full tuition
  • Visa application support

Research Grants

For postgraduate and doctoral research projects with demonstrable impact.

  • Project-based funding
  • Conference & publication support

Alumni & Endowed Awards

Endowed by alumni and partners to support future generations of KIU students.

  • Named scholarships
  • Programme-specific

Work-Study Programme

On-campus employment opportunities for eligible students to help fund their education.

  • Flexible hours
  • Career-building experience

Ready to Apply for a Scholarship?

Scholarship applications are submitted alongside your admission application. Indicate your interest during the process.
