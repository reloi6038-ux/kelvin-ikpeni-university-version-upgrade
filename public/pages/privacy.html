Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (name, email, phone, application details), information collected automatically (IP address, browser type, cookies), and information from third parties where relevant (e.g., academic records you ask us to access).

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to process applications, deliver academic services, communicate with you, improve our website, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers (hosting, email delivery), regulatory bodies where required, and partners strictly to deliver services you request.

4. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit, password hashing, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; report concerns to privacy@kiu.edu.ng.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and academic record-keeping requirements. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and security. Analytics and non-essential cookies are only set with your consent where required.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@kiu.edu.ng.
